# Dharampasha upazila, Sunamganj district (SUN_DHARMAPASHA)

## Land use, 2014-15 (BRRI regional survey)

Net cropped area 35,500 ha: single-cropped 30,910, double 3,750, triple 550 ha; cropping intensity 114%.

## Where the region's dominant rotations sit in this upazila

| Rotation | Area (ha) | % of upazila cropland |
|---|---|---|
| Boro-Fallow-Fallow | 29,500 | 83.1 |
| Boro-Fallow-T. Aman | 2,500 | 7.0 |
| Fallow-Fallow-T. Aman | 1,100 | 5.5 |

Crop diversity: 14 rotations and 12 crops recorded; crop diversity index 0.45.

## Farms and cattle (district)

273,369 farm holdings, 84% small (under 2.5 acres); 24% of owner-operators rent some land; 191,778 farm-labour households (Agriculture Census 2019).
 About 477,678 cattle (128 per km²; FAO GLW4, 2015).

Irrigated area 2022-23: 553 thousand acres, of which Boro 515, Aman 13, wheat 1, potato 3.
