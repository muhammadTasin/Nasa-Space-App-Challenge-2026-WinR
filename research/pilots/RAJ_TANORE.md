# Tanor upazila, Rajshahi district (RAJ_TANORE)

## Land use, 2014-15 (BRRI regional survey)

Net cropped area 22,550 ha: single-cropped 450, double 15,850, triple 5,980 ha; cropping intensity 224%.

Crop diversity: 16 rotations and 11 crops recorded; crop diversity index 0.90.

## Farms and cattle (district)

354,300 farm holdings, 92% small (under 2.5 acres); 25% of owner-operators rent some land; 279,865 farm-labour households (Agriculture Census 2019).
 About 477,930 cattle (197 per km²; FAO GLW4, 2015).

Irrigated area 2022-23: 693 thousand acres, of which Boro 166, Aman 188, wheat 56, potato 88.
