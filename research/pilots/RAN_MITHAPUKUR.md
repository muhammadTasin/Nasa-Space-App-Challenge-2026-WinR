# Mithapukur upazila, Rangpur district (RAN_MITHAPUKUR)

## Land use, 2014-15 (BRRI regional survey)

Net cropped area 44,530 ha: single-cropped 1,510, double 26,320, triple 15,950 ha; cropping intensity 231%.

## Farms and cattle (district)

396,196 farm holdings, 94% small (under 2.5 acres); 36% of owner-operators rent some land; 282,179 farm-labour households (Agriculture Census 2019).
 About 749,115 cattle (312 per km²; FAO GLW4, 2015).

Irrigated area 2022-23: 552 thousand acres, of which Boro 324, Aman 46, wheat 5, potato 104.
