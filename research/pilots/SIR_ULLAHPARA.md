# Ullapara upazila, Sirajganj district (SIR_ULLAHPARA)

## Land use, 2014-15 (BRRI regional survey)

Net cropped area 32,090 ha: single-cropped 5,440, double 18,070, triple 8,390 ha; cropping intensity 209%.

Crop diversity: 26 rotations and 18 crops recorded; crop diversity index 0.90.

## Farms and cattle (district)

373,984 farm holdings, 92% small (under 2.5 acres); 27% of owner-operators rent some land; 263,396 farm-labour households (Agriculture Census 2019).
 About 518,276 cattle (216 per km²; FAO GLW4, 2015).

Irrigated area 2022-23: 581 thousand acres, of which Boro 344, Aman 153, wheat 6, potato 4.
