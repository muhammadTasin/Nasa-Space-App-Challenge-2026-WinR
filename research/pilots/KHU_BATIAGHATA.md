# Batiaghata upazila, Khulna district (KHU_BATIAGHATA)

## Land use, 2014-15 (BRRI regional survey)

Net cropped area 17,870 ha: single-cropped 5,500, double 11,740, triple 500 ha; cropping intensity 172%.

## Farms and cattle (district)

251,331 farm holdings, 91% small (under 2.5 acres); 21% of owner-operators rent some land; 153,439 farm-labour households (Agriculture Census 2019).
 About 307,275 cattle (70 per km²; FAO GLW4, 2015).

Irrigated area 2022-23: 190 thousand acres, of which Boro 147, Aman 6, wheat 0, potato 1.
